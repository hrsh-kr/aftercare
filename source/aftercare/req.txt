# Problem Statement: Ownership Records Are Broken

## Background

Modern consumers own dozens of products that come with important documents:

* Purchase invoices
* Warranty cards
* Serial numbers
* Installation records
* Service history
* User manuals

These documents are essential throughout a product's lifecycle. They are needed for warranty claims, repairs, insurance, resale, support requests, and ownership verification.

Yet there is no simple, universal place where people can store and retrieve them.

---

## The Current Experience

After purchasing a product, ownership information becomes fragmented.

A typical purchase generates multiple pieces of information:

* A paper bill
* A PDF invoice
* A warranty card
* A support phone number
* A service record
* A serial number

Over time, these records become scattered across:

* WhatsApp chats
* Email inboxes
* Download folders
* Physical files
* Retailer portals
* Brand websites

When users actually need this information months or years later, they often cannot find it.

---

## Real-World Examples

### Warranty Claim

A customer purchases an air conditioner.

Ten months later it stops working.

To claim warranty, they need:

* Invoice
* Purchase date
* Model number
* Serial number

The invoice is buried in email, the warranty card is lost, and the serial number is difficult to locate.

A simple support request becomes frustrating.

---

### Appliance Service

A family owns multiple appliances:

* AC
* Washing machine
* Refrigerator
* Water purifier

Service records are spread across SMS messages, WhatsApp chats, and paper receipts.

No centralized history exists.

---

### Resale

A user wants to sell a laptop.

Potential buyers ask for:

* Original invoice
* Warranty status
* Purchase date

The documents cannot be found, reducing trust and resale value.

---

## Why Existing Solutions Fail

### Cloud Storage Is Generic

Tools like cloud drives can store files but do not understand ownership.

An invoice is treated the same way as any other PDF.

Users must manually:

* Organize folders
* Name files
* Remember locations

The burden remains on the user.

---

### Brand Apps Are Fragmented

Each manufacturer maintains its own ecosystem.

A user may own products from:

* Samsung
* LG
* Dell
* Daikin
* Philips

There is no unified ownership view.

---

### Retailer Portals Are Temporary

Invoices may be accessible through retailer websites, but:

* Users forget accounts
* Retailers change systems
* Purchases are distributed across multiple sellers

Ownership records remain fragmented.

---

## The Core Insight

The problem is not document storage.

The problem is ownership memory.

People do not want to manage files.

They want to know:

* When did I buy this?
* Is it still under warranty?
* Where is the invoice?
* What is the serial number?
* Who do I contact for support?

Current tools store documents.

No tool maintains a structured record of ownership.

---

## Vision

Every product a user owns should have a single, searchable record.

A user should be able to open one app and instantly see:

* Product details
* Purchase information
* Warranty timeline
* Attached documents
* Service history

without searching emails, folders, WhatsApp chats, or physical files.

---

## Product Principle

The product should be as simple as Splitwise.

Users should not need to:

* Create folders
* Manage files
* Enter extensive metadata

The experience should be:

Buy product
→ Add invoice
→ Product record created
→ Ownership information available forever

The application becomes a personal passbook for everything a user owns.

---

## Success Metric

The ultimate measure of success is not how many documents are uploaded.

It is whether users trust the application as their default source of truth for ownership information.

When a user asks:

"Where is the invoice for my AC?"

the answer should be:

"Open the app."
