# AquaSpin — Warranty Terms & Conditions

*Applies to all AquaSpin washing machine products.*

---

## 1. Warranty Period

- Motor and drum mechanism: 2 years from date of purchase.
- All other parts (filter assembly, door, control panel, hoses): 1 year from date of purchase.

Warranty is tied to the product's serial number and the original purchaser's registered details. It is not transferable to a subsequent owner unless the product is re-registered with proof of resale.

## 2. What Is Covered

- Manufacturing defects in the covered components above.
- Mechanical or electrical faults arising from normal household use, when the product was installed and maintained per the user manual.
- One free service visit per warranty year for a covered issue.

## 3. What Is Not Covered

- Damage from incorrect installation not following the user manual (e.g., a machine not leveled per Section 1).
- Damage from voltage fluctuation, unless the customer used a recommended stabilizer (sold separately).
- Cosmetic wear (scratches, fading, discoloration) that does not affect function.
- Damage from unauthorized repair attempts or use of non-AquaSpin replacement parts.
- Filter-related issues where the customer has not performed the maintenance described in the manual (e.g., odor caused by a lint filter left uncleaned for several months).
- Physical damage from external causes (falls, impact, water damage from flooding, fire).

## 4. Claim Process

1. Customer reports the issue with product serial number and a description of the fault.
2. AquaSpin support verifies warranty status against the registered purchase record.
3. For issues resolvable by self-service troubleshooting, support walks the customer through this first, per the product manual.
4. For issues requiring a technician visit, support schedules one within 5 business days in metro areas, 10 business days elsewhere.
5. If the technician determines the fault is not covered under Section 3, the customer is informed of repair cost before any chargeable work begins.

## 5. Safety

AquaSpin does not cover, and support will never attempt to troubleshoot over chat, any issue involving sparking, burning smell, smoke, exposed wiring, or water pooling near electrical components. These are always escalated to an emergency technician visit, at no cost to the customer regardless of warranty status, because they are safety issues first and warranty issues second.
