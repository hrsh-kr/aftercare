# Windmere Cyclone 1200 — Ceiling Fan
## User Manual

*Model: WM-CYC-1200 · 1200mm sweep · 3-speed*

---

## 1. Box Contents

- 1x Fan body with blades pre-attached
- 1x Down rod (300mm)
- 1x Ceiling mounting bracket
- 1x Regulator switch (3-speed)
- Mounting screws and fasteners
- This manual

---

## 2. Installation

1. Switch off mains power at the breaker before starting.
2. Fix the mounting bracket to a ceiling hook rated for at least 15kg.
3. Attach the down rod to the bracket, then the fan body to the down rod. The fan should hang at least 2.4m above the floor.
4. Connect the three wires — live (red), neutral (black), earth (green/yellow) — matching the colors to your house wiring. Use a wire tester before touching any exposed wire.
5. Wire the regulator switch inline with the live wire.
6. Restore power and test all three speeds before replacing the ceiling cover plate.

---

## 3. Operation

The regulator has three speeds. Speed 1 is lowest, Speed 3 is highest. Allow 2-3 seconds between switching speeds for the motor to adjust.

Recommended speed by room size:
- Small room (under 120 sq ft): Speed 1-2
- Medium room (120-200 sq ft): Speed 2
- Large room (over 200 sq ft): Speed 2-3

---

## 4. Maintenance

- Clean blades monthly with a dry or slightly damp cloth. Do not use water directly on the motor housing.
- Check blade screws every 6 months — vibration can loosen them over time.
- Do not oil or lubricate the motor. It is sealed and maintenance-free for its rated life.

## 5. Troubleshooting

### 5.1 Grinding or scraping noise

A grinding or scraping noise almost always means the blades are misaligned or a mounting screw has loosened, not a motor fault. Check:
- All blade screws are tight (see Section 4).
- The down rod is fully seated in the mounting bracket — a loose down rod connection causes a scraping sound at higher speeds specifically.
- No wiring or cover plate is touching the blades as they rotate.

If the grinding noise is present even with the fan switched off but swaying (e.g., from wind through a window), this is a mechanical bracket issue, not electrical, and is covered under the standard mechanical warranty.

### 5.2 Clicking noise

A rhythmic clicking that matches the blade rotation speed usually means a blade is slightly bent or a balancing weight (fitted to one blade at the factory) has shifted. This is a balancing issue, not a motor fault. Rebalancing is a covered service under warranty.

### 5.3 Humming noise at low speed only

Humming that occurs only at Speed 1 and disappears at Speed 2-3 is normal capacitor behavior in this model and does not indicate a fault.

### 5.4 Fan does not start, no sound at all

Check the regulator switch and the breaker first. If both are fine and the fan still does not respond at any speed, this indicates a capacitor or motor winding fault and requires a technician visit — this is not user-serviceable.

### 5.5 Burning smell, sparking, or visible smoke

**Stop use immediately. Switch off power at the breaker, not just the regulator. Do not attempt any troubleshooting or inspection yourself.** This indicates a serious electrical fault. Contact support immediately for an emergency service visit.

### 5.6 Fan wobbles visibly during rotation

Usually caused by uneven blade spacing or a bent blade. Measure the distance from each blade tip to the ceiling — they should be within 6mm of each other. A blade more than 6mm off should be gently reshaped or replaced, not forced straight while the fan is powered.
