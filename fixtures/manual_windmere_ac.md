# Windmere CoolBreeze 1.5T — Split AC
## User Manual

*Model: AC-CB-15T · 1.5 ton · Split, indoor + outdoor unit*

---

## 1. Installation

Installed by a Windmere technician at time of purchase. Outdoor unit needs at least 30cm clearance on all sides for airflow — do not enclose it or stack items against it.

---

## 2. Operation

Set temperature 24-26°C for typical efficient cooling. Cooling takes longer, and the unit works harder, at very low set temperatures on a hot day — this is normal, not a fault.

---

## 3. Maintenance

- Clean the indoor unit's air filter every 2-4 weeks in normal use, every 2 weeks in dusty conditions or during peak summer — a clogged filter is the single most common cause of both poor cooling and higher electricity bills.
- Filters are the two mesh panels behind the front flap of the indoor unit — slide them out, rinse with lukewarm water, air-dry fully before reinserting. Do not run the AC with a wet filter.
- Keep the outdoor unit's coil area clear of leaves, dust buildup, and obstruction.

---

## 4. Troubleshooting

### 4.1 Not cooling properly / blowing warm or weak air

Almost always a filter issue, checkable without any tools:

1. **Turn off the AC. Remove and check both filters** (Section 3). If visibly dusty, clean them per Section 3 and let them dry fully before reinstalling.
2. **Check the outdoor unit** isn't blocked or enclosed.
3. **Restart and give it 15-20 minutes** at a normal set temperature (24-26°C) before judging cooling performance.

If cooling is still poor after a clean filter and a clear outdoor unit, especially if you notice ice forming on the indoor unit's coil (visible through the front flap), turn the unit off and let any ice fully melt before running it again — do not chip or scrape ice off. If ice keeps returning, or cooling doesn't improve at all after these checks, this indicates a refrigerant or compressor issue and needs a technician visit.

### 4.2 Foul or musty smell when the AC is running

Usually caused by moisture sitting in the drain system, growing mold on the indoor coil, rather than anything wrong with the unit itself:

1. **Clean the air filters** (Section 3) — a dirty filter contributes to moisture buildup on the coil behind it.
2. **Check the drain pipe** (the thin pipe running from the indoor to outdoor unit, or to a drain point) isn't blocked — if water isn't draining and you notice dripping indoors, this is very likely the cause.
3. **Run the fan-only mode for 30 minutes** with cooling off, if available, to help dry out the coil.

If the smell persists after a filter clean, or if you're seeing indoor dripping alongside the smell, this needs a professional coil and drain clean — escalate rather than continuing to run the unit.

### 4.3 Water dripping from the indoor unit

Usually a blocked drain pipe (see 4.2, step 2). If clearing an accessible external section of the drain pipe doesn't stop it, or you can't safely access the pipe, escalate — this is not something to keep attempting indoors near electrical components.

### 4.4 Burning smell, sparking, unusual smell resembling gas, or visible smoke

**Stop use immediately. Switch off power at the breaker, not just the remote. Do not attempt any troubleshooting yourself.** Contact support immediately for an emergency visit — this applies regardless of warranty status.
