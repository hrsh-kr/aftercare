# AquaSpin 700 — Front-Load Washing Machine
## User Manual

*Model: WM-FC-700 · 7kg · Front load*

---

## 1. Installation

1. Place on a hard, flat floor — not carpet. Use the adjustable feet to level the machine; never use cardboard, folded paper, or a coin under a foot to correct unevenness, this makes vibration worse, not better.
2. Leave at least 5cm clearance on all sides for airflow and hose access.
3. Connect the inlet hose to a tap and the outlet hose to a drain at least 60cm off the floor.

---

## 2. Operation

Recommended load: do not exceed 7kg dry weight. Distribute clothes evenly around the drum before starting — a lopsided load is the single most common cause of noise and shaking during spin.

---

## 3. Maintenance

- Clean the lint filter (bottom-front, behind the small hatch) before every 4-5 washes.
- Run a drum-clean cycle with descaling powder once a month, or every 2 weeks in hard-water areas — mineral buildup on the drum and heating element is the leading cause of persistent odor and residue on dark clothing.
- Leave the door and detergent drawer open after each wash to let the drum dry out fully.

---

## 4. Troubleshooting

### 4.1 Drum wobbling or banging noise during spin

This is almost always a load or leveling issue, not a fault, and can usually be checked without any tools:

1. **Check the machine is level.** Rock it gently from each corner — if it rocks, adjust the feet until it doesn't. A machine that isn't level will shake even with a perfectly balanced load.
2. **Check the load is evenly spread.** Pause the machine, open the door, and spread the clothes around the drum by hand so they're not bunched on one side — this alone resolves the large majority of noise complaints.
3. **Re-run a cycle** after both checks and confirm whether the noise is still there.

If the noise continues after both checks, and especially if you can physically move the drum side-to-side by hand when the machine is off, this indicates worn bearings or a worn shock absorber — this is a mechanical fault, not something to continue troubleshooting at home, and should go to a technician. It is covered under the mechanism warranty (see Terms).

### 4.2 Foul smell or dirt/residue left on clothes after washing

Caused by a blocked lint filter (traps hair and fabric fibres, creating a damp environment where odor-causing residue builds up and gets redeposited on the next load) or mineral buildup in the drum from hard water:

1. **Clean the lint filter** (Section 3) — remove it, rinse thoroughly under running water, and reinsert firmly.
2. **Run a descaling cycle** (Section 3) if the filter was already clean or the smell persists — this clears mineral buildup a filter clean alone won't fix.
3. **Leave the door open** between washes going forward to prevent this recurring.

If the smell persists after both a filter clean and a descaling cycle, this may indicate a drain hose or trap blockage further inside the machine and should be escalated for a technician visit.

### 4.3 Machine won't start / no power

Check the plug, the socket (test with another appliance), and that the door is fully latched — most models won't start with the door not fully closed. If all three check out and it still won't start, this is not user-serviceable and should be escalated.

### 4.4 Water leaking, burning smell, or exposed/sparking wiring near the machine

**Stop use immediately. Switch off power at the socket, not just the machine's own power button. Do not attempt any troubleshooting yourself.** Contact support immediately for an emergency visit — this applies regardless of warranty status.
