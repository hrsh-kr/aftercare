# Windmere Appliances — Warranty Terms & Conditions

*Applies to all Windmere ceiling fan products, including the Cyclone 1200 series.*

---

## 1. Warranty Period

- **Motor**: 3 years from the date of purchase, as shown on the original invoice.
- **All other parts** (blades, regulator, down rod, mounting bracket): 1 year from the date of purchase.
- Warranty is tied to the product's serial number and the original purchaser's registered details. It is not transferable to a subsequent owner unless the product is re-registered with proof of resale.

## 2. What Is Covered

- Manufacturing defects in the motor, blades, regulator, or mounting hardware.
- Mechanical faults arising from normal household use, including blade balancing issues and bracket-related noise, when the product was installed per the manual's installation instructions.
- One free service visit per warranty year for a covered issue.

## 3. What Is Not Covered

- Damage from incorrect installation not following Section 2 of the user manual (e.g., inadequate ceiling hook rating, incorrect wiring).
- Damage from voltage fluctuation, unless the customer used the recommended stabilizer (sold separately).
- Cosmetic wear (scratches, fading) that does not affect function.
- Damage from unauthorized repair attempts or use of non-Windmere replacement parts.
- Any fault reported more than 30 days after the customer first noticed it, where the delay itself caused further damage.
- Physical damage from external causes (falls, impact, water damage from flooding, fire).

## 4. Claim Process

1. Customer reports the issue with product serial number and a description of the fault.
2. Windmere support verifies warranty status against the registered purchase record.
3. For issues resolvable by troubleshooting, support attempts this first.
4. For issues requiring a technician visit, support schedules one within 5 business days in metro areas, 10 business days elsewhere.
5. If the technician determines the fault is not covered under Section 3, the customer is informed of repair cost before any chargeable work begins.

## 5. Safety

Windmere does not cover, and support will never attempt to troubleshoot over chat, any issue involving sparking, burning smell, smoke, or exposed wiring. These are always escalated to an emergency technician visit, at no cost to the customer regardless of warranty status, because they are safety issues first and warranty issues second.
